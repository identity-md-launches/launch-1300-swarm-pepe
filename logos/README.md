# Swarm Pepe ($SPEPE) logo options

1. `logo-1.png` — Mascot: an original cheerful lime tree frog with winglike cheeks for the swarm.
2. `logo-2.png` — Geometric icon: three bold interlocking frog facets; selected as `../artifacts/logo.png`.
3. `logo-3.png` — Lettermark: heavy SPEPE typography with frog-eye shapes integrated into the P letters.
4. `logo-4.png` — Minted emblem: a lime frog coin with a broad gold rim and indigo relief.
5. `logo-5.png` — Meme illustration: an exuberant original frog with coral tongue and swarm motion bolts.

All five were drawn with the built-in image generation tool, one draft per option (five generated drafts total), then resized to 1024×1024 PNG without changing their artwork. No code-drawn fallback was used. All options have opaque backgrounds and were visually inspected at 64 px on white and dark surfaces and as 32 px circles.

All five drafts are retained as requested. Option 2 is the selected wallet mark: one strong symbol, no text or frame, edge-to-edge indigo background. Option 1 has more facial detail; option 3 contains lettering; option 4 has a coin rim and fine relief; option 5's motion marks and expression crowd a 32 px circle. Those tradeoffs make them less suitable for the selected wallet image. No additional wallet-logo redraws were needed.

The prompt set requested, respectively: an original simple tree-frog mascot; an interlocking geometric frog symbol; a bold SPEPE lettermark; a minted lime-and-gold frog badge; and a loud illustrated frog meme. Shared constraints were square format, indigo and electric-lime palette, opaque background, small-size readability, no existing characters or brands, and no text outside the lettermark.
